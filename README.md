## About Flume Voice

**Flume** (also searched as **Flume Voice** or **Flume AI**) turns messy spoken thought into clean, structured writing in any application on your computer or phone — 4x faster than typing.

### Key Capabilities
- **Universal Voice Dictation:** Press and hold your hotkey (`Fn` on macOS, `Ctrl+Win` on Windows, or the native Flume Voice Keyboard on iOS and Android) to dictate into Slack, Gmail, Notion, Cursor, VS Code, Linear, Word, or any text field.
- **Context-Aware AI Cleanup:** Automatically strips filler words (*um*, *uh*, *like*), resolves mid-sentence self-corrections, and adapts tone to the active app.
- **Bot-Free AI Meeting Notes:** Transcribes Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Slack Huddles directly from system + mic audio with zero meeting bots joining your call, complete with live action items and structured summaries.
- **Personal Dictionary & Auto-Learn:** Learns your names, jargon, and project terms automatically across desktop and mobile.
